### Medium finding

**Location:** `internal/syncer/translated.go:477-484`

For an earlier conversion that normalized a tree mode (for example, `100011` to `100644`), the origin seed loaded at `internal/syncer/translated.go:159-165` identifies the original commit, but its converted tree does not translate back to the original tree hash. An empty post-cutover commit reusing that converted tree has its tree excluded here even though the SHA1 target lacks the translated tree, causing receive-pack to reject the sync for missing objects. Include boundary-tree translations unless their target identities are known to exist, rather than assuming every seeded commit's trees round-trip.
